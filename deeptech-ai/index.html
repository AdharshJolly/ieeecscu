About this event

"IEEE DeepTech.AI" is an annual flagship symposium series organized by the IEEE Computer Society Bangalore Chapter that focuses on current challenges and research frontiers in Artificial Intelligence (AI).

While Generative AI unlocked unprecedented creative potential, the next phase of enterprise transformation is being driven by Agentic AI: intelligent systems capable of autonomous reasoning, complex planning, and decisive action across multi-step workflows. This is the shift from AI as a tool to AI as a collaborative digital employee.

This symposium is dedicated to moving beyond pilots and prototypes to address the critical challenges and strategies required to deploy, govern, and scale autonomous AI agents effectively across the entire enterprise stack.

The Paradigm Shift: From Automation to Autonomy - Agentic AI represents a strategic imperative, not just a technological upgrade. Enterprises leveraging autonomous agents are already reporting significant gains. We will explore how this technology moves core functions from static systems to dynamic ecosystems that optimize and adapt instantaneously.

Core topics

Practical, actionable content covering the technical, product and governance dimensions required to design, deploy and scale agentic AI across organisations.

  • Designing and building Multi‑Agent Systems (MAS)
  • Agent architecture & orchestration patterns
  • Governance, verification, trust and explainability
  • Safety guardrails, monitoring & oversight
  • Human‑in‑the‑Loop (HITL) interaction design
  • Change management for human‑agent collaboration
  • High‑value enterprise use cases and ROI
  • Measuring productivity gains with Agentic AI

Who should attend

This symposium is for practitioners and decision‑makers who want to move beyond pilots and build reliable, governed agentic systems:

  • AI/ML engineers and architects
  • Product managers and engineering leaders
  • Researchers and faculty exploring agents
  • Enterprise risk, compliance and governance teams
  • Students and early‑career professionals

Expect a mix of expert talks, panels and hands‑on workshops that equip you to design, evaluate and scale agentic AI.

FAQs

Have questions? We have answers. Browse through our most frequently asked questions below.

What is DeepTech.AI 2025?

IEEE DeepTech.AI is an annual flagship symposium series organized by the IEEE Computer Society Bangalore Chapter. This year's edition focuses on Agentic AI at Scale, exploring how autonomous AI agents are transforming enterprises from static automation to dynamic, adaptive ecosystems.

When and where is the event?

The symposium will be held on Friday, November 28th, 2025, from 9:00 AM to 4:30 PM at the Auditorium, Block I, CHRIST University, Kengeri Campus, Bangalore. A post-symposium workshop on building agents will run from 2:30 PM to 4:30 PM for interested participants.

Who should attend this symposium?

This event is ideal for AI/ML engineers and architects, product managers, engineering leaders, researchers, faculty members exploring agentic systems, enterprise risk and compliance teams, students, and early-career professionals who want to understand Agentic AI fundamentals and build enterprise-scale AI agents.

What topics will be covered?

The program covers building Multi-Agent Systems (MAS), agent architecture and orchestration patterns, governance and trust frameworks, safety guardrails and monitoring, Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) design, change management for human-agent collaboration, high-value enterprise use cases, ROI measurement, and productivity gains with Agentic AI.

What does the event format include?

The symposium features expert talks from industry leaders including Dr. Srinivas Padmanabhuni (Co-founder and CTO of testAIng) and Anshul Jain (Associate Director, MSD), a panel discussion on AI agent readiness, networking lunch, and a hands-on workshop on building agents. The workshop provides practical experience in developing autonomous AI agents.
